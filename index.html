Foundational & Modern Sociology

The Thinkers' Gallery

Click any sociologist card to open comprehensive study notes, key terms, and exam application pointers.

Classical Theorist

Émile Durkheim

Focused on social facts, division of labour, suicide typology, and religion as collective consciousness.

Conflict Theorist

Karl Marx

Examined historical materialism, modes of production, class struggle, and four dimensions of alienation.

Interpretive Sociology

Max Weber

Introduced Verstehen, social action, ideal types, bureaucracy, and the Protestant Ethic thesis.

Structural Anthropology

A.R. Radcliffe-Brown

Developed structural-functionalism comparing social institutions to biological organisms.

Ethnographic Method

Bronislaw Malinowski

Championed participant observation, Kula ring exchange, and psychological needs functionalism.

Indian Sociology

M.N. Srinivas

Pioneered field-view village studies, Sanskritisation, Westernisation, and Dominant Caste.

Social Prism is an educational initiative dedicated to making Sociology accessible, engaging, and meaningful for students and aspiring civil servants. Founded by Pathan Musheer Khan, M.A. in Sociology from Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), Pune, Social Prism aims to bridge the gap between sociological understanding and its practical application in academics and competitive examinations.

We believe that Sociology is more than the study of society — it is a way of understanding human behaviour, social institutions, inequalities, cultural transformations, and the forces that shape our everyday lives.

Core Concepts & Thinkers

Quick revision notes for Sociology.

Émile Durkheim

Key concepts: social facts, collective conscience, mechanical and organic solidarity, anomie, division of labour and sociology of religion.

Karl Marx

Key concepts: historical materialism, class struggle, modes of production, alienation, ideology and capitalism.

Max Weber

Key concepts: social action, ideal types, authority, bureaucracy, rationalisation and Protestant ethic.

M. N. Srinivas

Key concepts: Sanskritisation, Westernisation, dominant caste and field-view of Indian society.

Talcott Parsons

Structural functionalism, social system, pattern variables and AGIL framework.

Robert K. Merton

Middle-range theory, manifest and latent functions, dysfunctions and strain theory.
